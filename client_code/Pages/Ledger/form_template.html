Why doesn't the money go through the app?

Moving other people's money is regulated in Canada, so for now you pay each other directly: cash in hand or an Interac e-Transfer. No fees, no wallet to top up. The app keeps the record, the references and the dates, so there's a paper trail if anything goes wrong. In-app payments may come later.
