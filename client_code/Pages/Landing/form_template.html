Built against scams and no-pays

Cash jobs attract tricksters. Here's what stops them.

A scanner reads every bounty

Gift cards, crypto, cheques, "send the rest back", packages for strangers, roofs, wiring: flagged for a moderator before reaching the board.

Contact details hidden

Phone numbers, emails and links are blanked out until you're matched, so scammers can't pull you off the app.

Pay within 3 days

Once a job is validated, the poster can't post or hire again until they pay. Their pay record is public.

Checklists, not vibes

The score is what's ticked, with reasons for anything missed. No answer in 48 h? The helper's checklist counts.

Strikes and disputes

Moderators settle disputes. Upheld reports are strikes; three pause the account. Three reports pull a bounty off the board.

Reputation you can't fake

Records come from finished jobs only. Reviews are blind until both sides write them, and each neighbour counts once.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing. No fees for posting, no cut of the bounty. You pay your helper directly, in cash or by Interac e-Transfer.

Why not pay in the app?

Moving other people's money is regulated in Canada. Until a licensed payment partner is plugged in, you pay each other directly and the app keeps the record: amounts, references, dates.

How is the quality bonus decided?

With a checklist you write when you post (we suggest one for every task). You tick what was done; the share of ticked items is the score. If you offered "$10 at 80%", 4 of 5 items unlocks it. Unticking needs a reason the helper can read.

What if they don't pay?

Once a job is validated, the poster has 3 days to pay. Until they do, they can't post or hire. Helpers can open a dispute; a moderator decides, and a lost dispute is a strike. Three strikes pause the account. Every poster's profile shows how fast they pay.

Who sees my address?

Only the helper you pick, once you pick them. The board shows a neighbourhood name and a circle about 500 m wide.

Can teenagers help?

Yes, from 16. Shovelling and raking are classic first jobs. We ask anyone under 18 to get a parent's OK and to stick to outdoor jobs.

What jobs aren't allowed?

Anything that needs a licence (electrical, plumbing, gas, roofing, tree felling), childcare and rides, hazardous waste, buying things with the helper's money, gift cards and packages for strangers. A scanner sends anything risky to a moderator first.
