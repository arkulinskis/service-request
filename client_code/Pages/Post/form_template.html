How it works

  1. Post a bounty: what, where, how much (from $20).
  2. Neighbours send offers. You pick one; only they see your address.
  3. They send photo proof. You tick the checklist: that's the quality score.
  4. You pay cash or e-Transfer. Bonus if the score reaches your bar.
